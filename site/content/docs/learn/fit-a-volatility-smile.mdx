## What this calculates

`volatilitySurface` turns option quotes into a fitted implied-volatility surface. With one expiry,
that surface is a smile. `impliedVolatility(strike, expiry)` reads the fitted smile anywhere, the
slice exposes the fitted parameters, and `arbitrage()` checks the fit for butterfly and calendar
arbitrage.

## Choosing a model

- **SVI** (raw stochastic-volatility-inspired) fits total variance with five parameters and is the
  usual choice for equity smiles. It needs at least five strikes.
- **SABR** with β = 0.5 fits a stochastic-volatility model's implied-volatility approximation.
- **Monotone interpolation** (PCHIP) passes through every quote and makes no shape assumption beyond
  them.

## Reading the chart

Dots are your quotes and the line is the fitted smile, extended beyond the outermost strikes. Where
the line leaves the quotes, the model is extrapolating. The butterfly metric reports whether the
fitted smile implies a negative probability density anywhere on its check grid.
