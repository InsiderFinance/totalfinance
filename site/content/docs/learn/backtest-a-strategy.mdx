## What this calculates

`vectorized` runs a backtest from bars and a signal aligned to them. The signal says whether to hold
the asset on each bar; with `executionLag: 1` a signal observed on one bar trades on the next, so
the strategy can never act on a price before it exists. Fees, slippage, borrow costs, leverage
limits and the annualization basis are explicit options, and the report echoes them.

## Bring your own data

Choose **Use your data** in the panel and paste or upload daily bars as CSV or TSV with date, open,
high, low and close columns (volume is optional). Most broker and data-vendor exports work as they
are; rows newest-first are put in order and the panel tells you what it changed. Your data stays in
your browser.

## Reading the chart

Candles show each bar (hollow up, filled down), with the moving averages on top. Triangles mark
entries and exits on the bar where they fill. The equity and drawdown panes share the price axis, so
the crosshair lines up a trade with its effect.

## Honest limits

The rebalance rule here is every bar, so small trades keep the position at its target weight; the
fill count includes them. A good backtest on one series is not evidence of an edge. Read
[Backtesting](/guides/backtesting) for the engines, the laws every engine keeps and out-of-sample
practice.
