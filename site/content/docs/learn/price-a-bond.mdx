## What this calculates

`bonds.fixedRate` builds a bond with its full payment schedule. `yieldMetrics` prices it at a yield
and returns the clean and dirty price, accrued interest, Macaulay and modified duration, convexity
and DV01 (the price change for a one-basis-point rise in yield, per 100 of face value). The applied
day count, compounding and business-day convention are echoed in the assumptions.

## Reading the charts

The price–yield curve bends upward. That curvature is convexity: when yields fall, price rises more
than it falls when yields rise by the same amount. The cash-flow chart shows the coupons and the
final repayment of face value.
