## What this calculates

`strategy(legs, { multiplier })` assembles a position from ordinary leg records. `legs.call` and
`legs.put` take a strike, a per-share premium and a signed quantity (positive long, negative
short); `legs.stock` takes a price and a signed share count. `position.payoff()` returns the net debit
or credit, maximum profit and loss (or `null` when unbounded), the break-even prices and the P&L at
every requested underlying price.

## Reading the chart

The line is net profit or loss **at expiration**, including the premiums paid and received and the
100-share contract multiplier. Green shading is profit, red is loss, and dashed lines mark the
break-evens. Before expiry a position is worth something different: use
[Explore scenarios](/learn/explore-scenarios) to revalue it with time and volatility.

## Presets are just legs

The preset menu fills the table with ordinary legs. Their premiums are hypothetical Black–Scholes
values, not quotes. Edit any cell, add or remove legs, and the strategy is whatever the legs say: a
named preset never constrains a custom combination. The full catalogue of named positions is in
the [strategy guide](/guides/strategies).
