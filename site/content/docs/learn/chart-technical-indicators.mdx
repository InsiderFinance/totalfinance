## What this calculates

Every indicator is a function from a series to an ordinary array aligned with it: one value per bar,
or one named record for multi-output indicators such as Bollinger Bands (`upper`, `middle`, `lower`,
`bandwidth`, `percentB`) and MACD (`macd`, `signal`, `histogram`). Parameters are named, and
indicators with industry-standard defaults resolve them when you omit them.

## Warmup is not zero

An indicator cannot have a value before it has enough history. Those leading entries are `NaN`, and
the chart leaves them blank. They are not zeros and not signals; a strategy should treat them as
"no position". The [warmup guide](/guides/ta-warmup) lists the warmup of every registered
indicator.

## Bring your own bars

Paste or upload OHLC data in the panel. Indicators here use closing prices; the library also offers
high/low/volume indicators, streaming updates and snapshots for live charts. Browse the
[indicator registry](/guides/ta-indicators) and the [live charting guide](/guides/ta-live-charts).
