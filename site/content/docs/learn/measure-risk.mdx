## What this calculates

`valueAtRiskReport` estimates one-period value at risk and expected shortfall (conditional VaR) from
a return series. Both are positive loss magnitudes, as decimals of the position: `0.021` means a 2.1%
loss. The report echoes the confidence level, method and horizon, and its diagnostics explain any
adjustment it could not apply.

## Historical or parametric

- **Historical** takes the empirical tail quantile of your returns. It keeps fat tails and skew, but
  it can only see losses that are in the sample.
- **Parametric** fits a normal distribution's mean and volatility. It is smooth, but it assumes the
  tails are normal, which equity returns usually are not.

## Reading the charts

The histogram shades the returns at or below −VaR; expected shortfall is their average. The drawdown
chart compounds the returns in order and shows how far below its running peak the investment fell,
a path risk that a one-period VaR does not capture.
