## What this calculates

`discountedCashFlow` discounts a forecast of free cash flow plus a terminal value. Firm-basis cash
flows (FCFF) produce **enterprise value**, not equity value: no debt, cash or share count is invented
for you. `discountedCashFlowSensitivityTable` re-runs the same valuation for every pair of discount
rate and terminal growth, so each cell equals a direct `discountedCashFlow` call by construction.

## Reading the heatmap

Rows are discount rates and columns are terminal growth rates; darker cells are higher enterprise
values. The outlined cell is your base case. A valuation that swings widely across neighbouring
cells is telling you how much of the answer is assumption.

## Watch the terminal share

The **terminal share of value** metric reports how much of enterprise value comes from the terminal
value. When it dominates, the discount rate and terminal growth, which are assumptions rather than
market facts, drive the answer.
