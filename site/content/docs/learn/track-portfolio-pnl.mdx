## What this calculates

`createPortfolioLedger` turns dated events (deposits, fills, dividends, fees) into an immutable
ledger. `portfolioPnl` values that ledger at two marks and explains the change: realized and
unrealized P&L, income, costs, financing, foreign-exchange effects, external cash flows and a
reconciliation residual that should be zero.

## Why the waterfall matters

A deposit raises account value, but it is not profit. The waterfall keeps the two apart: investment
P&L and external flows are separate steps from the opening to the closing value. Change the deposit
in the panel and only the deposits step moves; investment P&L stays put.

## Your own portfolio

Replace the sample events with your own fills and cash movements, and the sample marks with your own
timestamped prices. TotalFinance never fetches prices: every valuation uses the market snapshot you
pass in, and the report carries the assumptions it used.
