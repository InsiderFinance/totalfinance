## What this calculates

`position.scenarioTable()` reprices every leg with a model for each combination of underlying
price, volatility shock and days forward. The result is mark-to-market P&L relative to the model
premiums paid today, not an expiration payoff and not a forecast.

## Reading the heatmap

Each cell is one scenario. Green cells make money and red cells lose it; the numbers are the P&L in
USD. Moving down the rows raises implied volatility; advancing the valuation date in the panel shows
time decay working through the whole grid.

## Compare with expiration

The [strategy analyzer](/learn/analyze-a-strategy) shows the same kind of position at expiration.
The difference between the two pictures is the time value still in the options.
