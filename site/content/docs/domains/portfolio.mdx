---
title: 'Portfolio'
description: 'Event-sourced ledgers, positions, lots, cash, P&L attribution and timelines.'
---

Record what happened as dated events (fills, deposits, income, costs, corporate actions) and derive everything else: positions, lots, cash, P&L between any two marks and a valuation timeline. The trade-lifecycle tools propose, preflight and reconcile rebalances without ever placing an order.
