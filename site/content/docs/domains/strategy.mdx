title: 'Strategies'
description: 'Multi-leg option positions: payoff, break-evens, scenarios and a named strategy catalogue.'
---

Compose positions from legs, or start from one of the named presets, then ask the position for its expiration payoff, scenario table, Greeks or a classification. `scanStrategies` and `optimizeStrategy` search a chain for candidates under explicit objectives.
