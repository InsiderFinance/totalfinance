## Precision policy and remaining limits

- Exact forward-ATM horizon volatility below 1e-4 uses a Gaussian integral expansion whose omitted relative term is below 5e-29. Other subtraction with two positive legs at/below `4096 × EPSILON × max(legs)` receives a typed precision refusal. This conservative supported-precision boundary can change success to refusal for exceptionally poorly resolved inputs; public types remain unchanged. It is not an arbitrary-precision pricer or a universal accuracy guarantee.
- The loan drift bound follows operation count, not currency cents, and cannot conceal the reported material unpaid balance. The final row payment is explicit and may differ from the level payment. Existing tests were updated to assert that bounded correction and to require honest refusals where a payment had rounded to interest alone.
- Positive Monte Carlo SE can accompany `confidenceInterval: null` and `converged: false` if the interval endpoints cannot be represented around the estimate. Exact zero-variance controls stay zero. Sampling uncertainty is not model uncertainty or proof of rare-event coverage.
- Finite IRR scanning retains its documented range and ambiguity behavior and cannot promise every root, especially without a sampled sign change. Changing units cannot restore cash-flow dynamic range lost during normalization.
- These repairs do not change options models, compounding, cash-flow timing, fill conventions, provider contracts or public exports.
