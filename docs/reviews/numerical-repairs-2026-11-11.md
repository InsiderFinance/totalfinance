## Precision policy and remaining limits

- Horizon volatility below 1e-4 uses the same Gaussian time-value integral for ATM and non-ATM inputs, expanded in horizon volatility. A positive normal Mills continued fraction evaluates tail time value without CDF-leg subtraction; log scaling preserves representable premiums even when a tail density alone would underflow. Independent 400-digit erfc references pin accepted near-ATM/tail boundaries. Spot=strike with equal carry (BSM), equal forward/strike (Black76), and zero-strike equal-spot/equal-yield Kirk exchanges retain the direct ATM expansion. Unresolvable cancellation when computing forward moneyness is still an explicit refusal. The regular-volatility subtraction threshold includes tail conditioning; it is not a universal relative-accuracy guarantee.
- The loan allowance propagates through compounding and cannot exceed 1e-10 of original principal. It cannot conceal the reported material unpaid balance. The final installment is explicit and may differ from the level payment; totals are binary64 sums of actual rows, not exact-decimal promises.
- Positive Monte Carlo SE may accompany `confidenceInterval: null` when endpoints cannot be represented. That reporting limit does not change convergence of a finite estimate/statistic. Exact zero-variance controls stay zero. Sampling uncertainty does not establish rare-event coverage.
- Finite IRR scanning retains its documented range and ambiguity behavior and cannot promise every root, especially without a sampled sign change. Every detected unresolved bracket appears in warning context and prevents a plain single-root answer. Residual convergence is relative to the sum of absolute discounted terms, not the input cash scale alone.
- These repairs do not change options models, compounding, cash-flow timing, fill conventions, provider contracts or public exports.
